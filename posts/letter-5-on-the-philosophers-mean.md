> Inwardly, we ought to be different in all respects, but our exterior should conform to society. [^1]

> Philosophy calls for plain living, but not for penance; and we may perfectly well be plain and neat at the same time.

> I find in the writings of our Hecato that the limiting of desires helps also to cure fears:
>
> “Cease to hope,” he says, “and you will cease to fear.”
