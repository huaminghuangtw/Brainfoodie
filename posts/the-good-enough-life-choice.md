> [There is no meaning in life. Life is just life. It has no inherit meaning at all. You just project it with whatever meaning that suits you. But the most important thing is **remembering to remove whatever meaning is holding you back**. Because they say humans are 75% of water, but I think humans are also 99% unused potential. And most of us, the thing holding you back from where you are to where you want to be is always in your head. And it’s usually because you put ‘meaning’ on something that isn’t there. So **if you learn that life has no meaning, you will do whatever you want.**](https://youtu.be/6aTaoGWjMvI?t=17m59s)
