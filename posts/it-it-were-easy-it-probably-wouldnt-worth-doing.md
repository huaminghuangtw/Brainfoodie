The note Melanie Perkins, founder of Canva, wrote to herself in the early days:

> Mel, you’re extremely tired. You’re in a challenging situation though you can pull through.
>
> Nothing bad is really happening. You’re just feeling depressed because you’re used to achieving things quickly. It’s a hard environment.
>
> There is no doubt you will succeed, and you will find the team you need, get the investment you need, and build the company you’ve always wanted.
>
> You have chosen to put yourself in a challenging situation.
>
> **If it wasn’t challenging you wouldn’t feel satisfied when you get to the end goal.**
