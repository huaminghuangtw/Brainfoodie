* Deliberate Relaxation
	* Just _shake/vibrate your limbs (arms & legs)_ [^1]
	* Be particularly aware of your _facial tension_
	* ⭐️ Jacobsen’s [Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR)](https://www.google.com/search?q=progressive+muscle+relaxation)
		* is a two-step relaxation practice, involving _tightening_ and _relaxing_ all of your major muscle groups.
		* It pretty much means lying down, progressively _tightening_ all your muscles, then _relaxing_ them, one at a time, in a particular/specific order/pattern.
* Do a _pleasant, non-exhausting_ series of exercises **in the morning**, like _joint rotations_, _arm swings_. Your ability to access the peak performance at a high level is gonna be greater later in the day.

[^1]: 甩手＋抖腳
